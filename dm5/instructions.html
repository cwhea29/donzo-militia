Map Guide
How the ops map works — zones, layers, clearance, and markers.
v3.0
01 Getting started

The map pulls locations from a live database — Los Santos, Cayo Perico, and Roxwood each have their own pins. When someone with the right clearance drops a marker, it shows up for everyone who can see it. No refresh needed.

  1. 1
    Sign in with your callsign and password. New to the crew? A Commander can add you from the Users panel.
  2. 2
    Pick a zone — Los Santos for the mainland, or Cayo Perico. Each zone keeps its own set of markers.
  3. 3
    Switch layers between Atlas, Roadmap, and Satellite depending on what you're planning.
  4. 4
    Click a marker to read details, leave comments, or edit if you have permission. Pins above your clearance level don't show up at all.

03 Map layers
🗺️
Atlas
Clean overview. Good default for most work.
🛣️
Roadmap
Street-level detail. Use this when you're routing vehicles or planning approaches.
🛰️
Satellite
Terrain and rooftops. Handy for off-road paths and building IDs.
!
Switching layers won't move your markers — pins stay exactly where they were placed. If map images fail to load, a stylized fallback kicks in and everything still works.
04 Access levels

Eleven clearance tiers control what you see and what you can do. If a location is above your level, the marker simply isn't there — not greyed out, not locked, just invisible.

Level 1 — Hangy
Minimal visibility. Mostly public pins.
Level 2 — Cadet
Basic locations only.
Level 3 — Private
A bit more than Cadet, still tight.
Level 4 — Gunner
Can place markers. Mid-tier locations visible.
Level 5 — Corporal
Place markers and delete your own.
Level 6 — Commander
Mid-high access. Can manage some locations.
Level 7 — Captain
Most sensitive pins visible.
Level 8 — Chief
Can delete most markers.
Level 9 — Warboss
Near-full access.
Level 10 — Underboss
Very high clearance.
Level 11 — Boss
Full admin. Sees everything, manages users, places and deletes any marker.

06 Groups, comments & heist plans

Groups — Tag locations with labels like "Casino" or "Cayo Runs." Filter from the sidebar or assign when placing a marker. Create new groups from the Locations panel.

Comments — Each popup has a live thread. Only the author can edit or delete their note. Good for quick updates: "heat's up," "cameras moved," "cleared."

Heist plans — Chain markers into ordered steps from the sidebar. Add, remove, reorder. Plans are shared across the org.

07 Placing a location

Gunner (Level 4) and above.

  1. 1
    Hit Place Marker in the toolbar. Cursor becomes a crosshair.
  2. 2
    Click the map where you want the pin. A form opens.
  3. 3
    Name it (required), add a description, photo, category, and the minimum clearance to view it.
  4. 4
    Save. It appears immediately for everyone with access — no reload.
→
Photos upload straight to Supabase Storage and serve over CDN. Groups help you filter later — e.g. tag a run as "Bank Jobs."
!
Double-check visibility before saving. Only a Commander+ can delete a misplaced pin. Wrong level? Ask someone to remove it and re-add.
08 User management

Commanders and up get the Users button in the toolbar — add members, change levels, remove people who've left. You can also edit users directly in Supabase under Table Editor → users.

!
Security note: Passwords are stored in plain text in the users table, and the Supabase anon key is in the client. Anyone inspecting the page can read/write within their clearance. Use strong, unique passwords. For production, switch to Supabase Auth with RLS policies and server-side user creation.
