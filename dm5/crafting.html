🧪 Common Production Lines
🧪
Methamphetamine
Inputs: Pseudoephedrine (pharmacy runs), Red Phosphorus, Iodine, Lithium strips (batteries), Acetone.
Kit: 2× Large Stove, 1× Acid Lab upgrade recommended.
Yield: ~18-24 units per 45min cook (varies with heat control).
Notes: High heat = boom risk. Keep multiple exits.
🌿
Weed / Marijuana
Inputs: Fertilizer, Water (clean), Seeds (Sour Diesel / OG Kush strains), Pots.
Kit: Grow tent or warehouse lights. 4-6 plants per cycle.
Yield: 12-20oz per harvest (7-9 day cycle).
Notes: Smell travels — use sealed locations or good ventilation cover.
❄️
Cocaine
Inputs: Coca paste (import), Baking soda / Acetone, Kerosene or Ether, Press.
Kit: Clean table + ventilation. Small batches safer.
Yield: 8-14 bricks from one paste run.
Notes: Extremely high value. Guard the lab.
🔫
Weapon Parts / Ammo
Common: 9mm / .45 / 5.56 / 7.62 ammo crates.
Inputs: Gunpowder, Brass casings, Primers, Gun parts (from heists or gun shops).
Yield: 180-250 rounds per crate batch.
Notes: Heavy armor + suppressors need higher level components.
🛡️
Body Armor & Gear
Inputs: Kevlar sheets, Ceramic plates, Ballistic fabric, Thread.
Kit: Workbench + sewing station.
Variants: Light vest (fast), Heavy plate (tank), Carrier (custom mods).
Notes: Always stock spares before big ops.
💊
Combat Stims & Meds
Inputs: Painkillers, Epinephrine, Bandages, Adrenaline.
Recipes: Adrenaline shot = 2× Epinephrine + syringe.
Armor repair kits and trauma kits always in demand.

⚠️ Safety & OPSEC
⛔
Never craft at main base. Use throwaway or rented locations. Always have at least one decoy site and an extraction plan. Log new labs on the map at "Base" or "Loot" with appropriate clearance.
🧠
Rotate suppliers weekly. Use low-level runners (Gunner+) for resupply. High value cooks should be run by Corporal+ only with spotters on perimeter.
🔗 Related Tools
Use the MAP to mark production sites, drop points, and buyer locations. Create a dedicated Heist Plan or Group called "Cook Runs" for fast coordination. Check MAP GUIDE for placement rules.
